Pin Cleaner gives you a calmer, more useful Pinterest.

HIDE WHAT YOU DON'T WANT
• Ads: hidden by default, everywhere on Pinterest, including search results and the "more like this" grid under a pin.
• AI-generated images: one click hides every pin Pinterest itself labels as AI modified.
• Video: one click hides video pins.

Hidden pins are removed before Pinterest lays out the page, so the grid stays packed with no empty holes.

FILTER BY COLOR AND SHAPE
Pick one or more of 12 colors and any of portrait, square or landscape. Only matching pins are shown, on any Pinterest page.

PICK, ORGANIZE, EXPORT
• Hover any pin and press + to pick it. Picked pins stay visible even when your filters would hide them.
• File picks into your own folders.
• Open the library to move, rename and review your picks.
• Download in full resolution: the original image, not the thumbnail. Save each one as a JPG or a whole folder as a ZIP.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Nothing is collected or sent anywhere. Your settings, picks and folders stay in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

Pin Cleaner is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.
