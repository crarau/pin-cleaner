# Pin Cleaner privacy policy

Pin Cleaner is a Chrome extension published by Ideaplaces Inc.

## What Pin Cleaner collects

Nothing. Pin Cleaner does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, browsing history or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking and no servers.

## What stays in your browser

- Your settings (which content to hide, your color and shape filters) are saved with Chrome's storage on your device.
- The images you pick, their titles and the folders you create are saved with Chrome's storage on your device.
- These stay on your computer until you remove them or uninstall the extension.

## What the permissions are for

- **Pinterest pages:** to hide ads, video and AI-generated pins and to apply your filters on the pages you visit.
- **i.pinimg.com (Pinterest's image server):** to download the full-resolution image when you export your picks.
- **Downloads:** to save exported JPGs and ZIP files to your computer.
- **Storage:** to keep your settings, picks and folders.
